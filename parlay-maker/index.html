How the edge works (read this once)

Simple: Sharp books like Pinnacle take huge limits from pros and move their lines fast, so their price is the closest thing to the "real" odds. Strip out their fee (the vig) → true win %. When a softer book (DK, FD, MGM…) pays more than that true price, that leg is +EV.

Why parlays: Parlay EV multiplies. Three legs with +3% edge each ≈ 1.03³ − 1 = +9.3% on the parlay. But three −4.5% legs (normal -110 juice) ≈ −13% — that's why random parlays bleed money.

Kelly: The stake that maximizes long-run bankroll growth is (b·p − q)/b. Full Kelly is wild on parlays, so default is ¼ Kelly.

Reality check: No tool guarantees wins. A 3-leg parlay at 18% hit chance loses ~4 of 5 times even with an edge. Edges are small, variance is huge, books limit winners. Bet only what you can lose. Problem? 1-800-GAMBLER.
