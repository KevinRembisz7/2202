How the edge works (read this once)

Simple: Sharp books like Pinnacle take huge bets from pros and move fast, so their price ≈ the real odds. Strip their fee (the vig) → true win %. When DK/FD/etc. pay more than that, the leg is +EV.

The matchup layer: the price edge is the math. The breakdown is the why — if the Jets are +EV and the Chiefs are missing two corners, that's a story that backs the number. If the edge exists because your book hasn't reacted to a QB being ruled out, the vibe goes 🔴 and you should stay away (the sharp line already knows).

Parlay math: edges multiply. Three +3% legs ≈ +9.3%. Three normal −110 legs ≈ −13%.

Kelly: stake = (b·p − q)/b of bankroll; default ¼ Kelly because parlays are high variance.

Reality check: nothing here guarantees wins. Injury notes are rules of thumb from ESPN data, not scouting. Bet small, bet what you can lose. 1-800-GAMBLER.
